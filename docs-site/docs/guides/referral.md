# Referral Program

Invite developers and teams to ADTY AI and earn commission on their qualified top-ups. Live program terms for the current site are displayed directly below.

## How Invite Links & Attribution Work

1. Open the **Referral** section in the console at [https://www.adtyai.com](https://www.adtyai.com).
2. Copy your unique referral link or invitation code and share it with colleagues, readers, or community members.
3. When a new user registers through your link (or enters your invite code during registration), the referral relationship is bound to their account.
4. Qualified paid top-ups made by your referred users—starting from their first qualifying order—generate first-tier direct commission at your active referral rate.

::: info Attribution Integrity
Make sure invitees sign up directly through your referral link in the same browser session or enter your invite code during sign-up. Attribution cannot be retroactively attached after an account has already completed orders without a referrer.
:::

## Observation Period (Freeze Window)

Newly generated commissions first appear as **pending / in observation** for the duration shown in the live summary card above:

- The observation period allows online payment settlements and refund or chargeback windows to clear safely.
- If the underlying top-up is refunded or reversed during the observation window, the corresponding pending commission is automatically canceled.
- Once the observation period finishes with no dispute, the commission automatically moves into your **available commission balance**.

## Redeeming Your Commission

You have two ways to use available commission, depending on site settings:

### 1. Transfer to Account Balance (Always Available)

- **Fee**: **0%**
- **Minimum amount**: **$0** (no minimum threshold)
- **Processing time**: **Instant**, with no manual approval required

Converted funds enter your USD wallet balance immediately and can be spent on any model API request right away.

### 2. External Cash Withdrawal (When Enabled)

When the current site has external cash withdrawals enabled (see the **Cash Withdrawal Methods** card at the top of this page):

- Supported payout rails (such as USDT on configured networks or Alipay), minimum withdrawal amount, and any fixed network/processing fees follow the live rules shown above and in your console.
- Submitting a withdrawal request requires account security verification (see [Account Security & PAT](/guides/security)) and passes through review before payout dispatch.
- Double-check your payout address and selected blockchain network carefully—on-chain transfers sent to an incorrect address or mismatched network cannot be reversed.

## Anti-Fraud & Fair Play Rules

To keep the referral program sustainable for genuine advocates, the following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

- **No self-invites**: Creating secondary accounts to invite yourself and collect rebates on your own top-ups.
- **No circular invites**: Coordinating mutual or ring-shaped referral loops across linked accounts or teams.
- **No abusive traffic**: Automated sign-up farming, fake order cycling, misleading claims, or deceptive brand impersonation.

::: danger Compliance Enforcement
Accounts detected engaging in self-referral, circular invite rings, or fraudulent billing activity will have pending and unpaid commissions forfeited and may lose referral eligibility or account access.
:::
