## What a Subsite Includes

ADTY AI Subsite Partnership gives resellers, studios, developer communities, and regional operators a turnkey branded deployment with full commercial autonomy:

### Independent White-Label Identity

- **Custom branding**: Configure your own site name, logo, favicon, homepage messaging, and announcements.
- **Branded legal pages**: Publish your own User Agreement, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy on your domain.
- **Auto-branded documentation**: The `/docs/` portal mounted on your subsite dynamically inherits your brand name, logo, console URL, dedicated API domain, live payment channels, and referral rules automatically.

### Custom Dual-Domain Architecture

- **Dedicated dual-domain setup**: Bind both your custom console domain (for example, `www.example.com`) and dedicated API gateway domain (for example, `api.example.com`) with TLS managed cleanly.
- **Clean traffic separation**: Your website domain serves the branded console and documentation portal while your dedicated API domain handles high-concurrency model relay traffic without interference.

### Independent Retail Commercial Controls

- **Retail pricing multiplier**: Set your own retail pricing multiplier and commercial margin independently of upstream supply settlement.
- **Recharge Bonus campaigns**: Configure USDT Recharge Bonus rates directly from your site admin panel.
- **Built-in Referral Program**: Toggle referrals on or off for your subsite, set your own commission percentage, observation period, and supported withdrawal methods.

### Complete Operational Suite

- **Team & staff sub-account management**: Assign dedicated staff operators with scoped admin permissions to handle daily operations safely.
- **Integrated support ticket center**: Manage customer tickets, screenshots, and replies directly inside your branded console.
- **Custom email delivery**: Connect your own branded email/SMTP settings for verification codes and transactional notifications.
- **Daily settlement sheets**: Track gross revenue, supply cost, bonuses, referral payouts, and net settlement transparently through daily reconciliation sheets.

## Reseller Tiers & Supply Discounts

Specific reseller tiers and supply discounts are currently customized—please contact customer support for details.

## Margin Deposit

Depending on your site tier and applicable platform operating rules, launching or upgrading a subsite may involve a **margin deposit**. In general terms, a margin deposit serves as an operational compliance deposit and, where applicable, an optional marketing coverage buffer supporting uninterrupted settlement and user protection.

Exact margin deposit requirements vary by partnership tier and current platform policy—please contact customer support for exact details before onboarding.
